About Jake Evans

I’m a father, husband, neighbor, and currently serve on the Neosho City Council.

My involvement in local government began in 2023, when I started attending City Council meetings to better understand the decisions being made in our community. Over time, that interest grew into a deeper commitment to public participation, transparency, and helping residents better understand how Neosho’s charter form of government works.

In April 2026, I was elected to serve on the Neosho City Council and was officially sworn into office on April 21, 2026. My current term runs through April 2030.

I created this website to help explain local government, the City Charter, the Code of Ordinances, Council actions, and civic processes in plain English. My goal is to make local government easier to follow so more residents can feel informed, confident, and prepared to participate.

This site is maintained by me personally. Although I serve on the Neosho City Council, this website is not an official City of Neosho publication and should not be viewed as speaking on behalf of the City or the Council as a whole.
